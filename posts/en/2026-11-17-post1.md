New information from the NBDC Human Database has been added as a Human Genome Dataset for the following diseases.

- [Xeroderma pigmentosum (NANDO:1200608)](https://nanbyodata.jp/disease/NANDO:1200608?lang=en#human-genomic-datasets): Added [hum0612](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en/research/hum0612)

#### Specific pediatric chronic disease
- [Xeroderma pigmentosum (NANDO:2201002)](https://nanbyodata.jp/disease/NANDO:2201002?lang=en#human-genomic-datasets): Added [hum0612](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en/research/hum0612)
- [Liposarcoma (NANDO:2200065)](https://nanbyodata.jp/disease/NANDO:2200065?lang=en#human-genomic-datasets): Added [hum0489](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en/research/hum0489)
