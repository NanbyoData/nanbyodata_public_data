We are pleased to announce the release of the Nanbyo Disease Ontology (NANDO) Version 2026-09-15.

- Main Updates
  - Added three newly designated intractable diseases following the April 2026 revision.
  - Updated the disease name for NANDO:1200755 from “Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency” to the revised Japanese term “α1－アンチトリプシン欠乏症.”
  - Fixed character encoding issues.
